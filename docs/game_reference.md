## POWER CARD TYPES

### At-Will Powers (reusable every turn)
Basic attacks — weapon strikes, cantrips, simple spells.
- Typically +6 to +8 attack bonus
- Deal 1-2 damage on hit
- Range: adjacent or within 1 tile

### Daily Powers (one-use, flip face-down)
Powerful attacks and dramatic effects.
- Typically +8 to +10 attack bonus  
- Deal 2-3 damage, often with additional effects
- May hit multiple targets or have area effects

### Utility Powers (one-use, flip face-down)
Support abilities — heals, buffs, movement tricks.
- Don't require attack action
- Trigger at specific times (e.g., "when attacked", "at start of Hero Phase")
- Effects: heal allies, grant movement, recharge powers, remove conditions

## TREASURE/ITEMS

### Item Types
1. **Blessings** — Immediate, temporary buff lasting until end of next turn (affects all heroes)
2. **Fortunes** — Immediate one-shot benefit, then discard
3. **Items** — Permanent equipment kept by a hero

### Common Treasure Cards (across games)
**Weapons:**
- Vorpal Sword (+3 attack, +1 damage)
- Flaming Sword (+2 attack)
- Frost Sword
- Holy Avenger
- Crossbow of Speed

**Armor/Defense:**
- Shield (+2 AC)
- Ring of Protection (+1 AC)
- Cloak of Protection
- Amulet of Warding

**Utility Items:**
- Potion of Healing (regain HP)
- Wand of Fireballs
- Ring of Shooting Stars
- Boots of Speed (+1 Speed)
- Thieves' Tools (bonus to disable traps)

**Fortunes/Blessings:**
- Blessing of Strength (+2 attack bonus until end of next turn)
- Fortune of Discovery (look at top 3 tiles, reorder)
- Blessing of Protection (+2 AC to all heroes until end of next turn)
- Level Up (spend 5 XP to become 2nd level)

### Gold Piece Items (Temple of Elemental Evil onwards)
Items have buy/sell prices. Gold earned from:
- Adventure completion rewards (100-200 GP typically)
- Selling unwanted Item Treasure cards
- Specific adventure bonuses

## ENCOUNTER CARD TYPES

1. **Events (Yellow)** — Strange occurrences, skill checks, narrative effects
2. **Event-Attacks (Red)** — Events that include an attack roll vs heroes
3. **Environments** — Global persistent effects (only one active at a time)
4. **Traps** — Placed on tiles, activate each Villain Phase

---

## SPELL DECK (Tomb of Annihilation onwards)

Spells are shared cards castable by Heroes and sometimes Monsters:
- Shuffled back into deck after use
- Card indicates different effects based on caster (Hero vs Monster)
- Examples: Acid Arrow, Mage Armor, Fire Shield, Pass Without Trace, Web, Wand of Wonder

---

## DUNGEON TILES

Each game includes ~32-40 standard tiles plus special/named tiles:
- **4×4 grid squares** per tile
- **White triangle** = safer (no encounter draw)
- **Black triangle** = dangerous (triggers encounter card draw)
- **Monster symbol** = place monster here when tile revealed
- **Trap symbol** = place trap token here
- **Treasure/Chest symbol** = place chest token (later games)
- **Named tiles** = used in specific adventures as objectives

## LEVELING UP

### Original (Castle Ravenloft / Wrath / Drizzt)
- Roll natural 20 on attack + spend 5 XP = Level 2
- **Benefits:** +2 HP, +1 AC, +1 Surge Value, new Daily power, critical hit ability

### Campaign Style (Temple onwards)
- Spend 1,000-2,000 GP during Town actions = Level 2
- Same stat benefits as above
- No XP spending required
